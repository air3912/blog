# Approved character design v1

This is the canonical design reference for future sprites and expression variants.

- Young-adult Galgame heroine with a warm, cute, approachable personality
- Soft chestnut tea-brown, shoulder-length hair with an airy silhouette and a small side braid
- Gray-teal eyes and a tiny warm-gold firefly hairpin
- Misty cornflower-blue fit-and-flare dress with a deep navy side ribbon
- Warm ivory blouse and cropped knit bolero
- Deep navy mini-pouch and rounded lace-up shoes
- Warm-gold firefly and floral embroidery near the skirt hem
- Modern Japanese light-novel / visual-novel line art with clean cel shading

Preserve the face, hair design, outfit cut, and palette when creating expressions or poses. The reference image is `character-design-v1.png`.
